    Surfaces, not apps

    There is no launcher or window zoo. Pip brings a surface up when asked, says why it opened it, and the face moves aside rather than disappearing.

    Every answer shows its cost

    A chip says whether an answer came from code or the model, and what it cost. That keeps the 0.8B model's speed limits visible instead of hidden.

    An honest body

    Each reading is tagged as a sensor or an estimate. The installer's hardware check names what is unknown, such as Wi-Fi that needs firmware you supply.

    Real forgetting, shown

    Forgetting walks through every place a memory lives, including what Pip worked out from it, and the journal line vanishes too.
